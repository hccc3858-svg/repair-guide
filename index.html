Dorm Repairs & Electricity in China 宿舍报修与用电指南

A practical guide for international students at Suzhou Polytechnic University who need help with a broken socket, light or air conditioner. 一份面向苏州职业技术大学国际学生的实用指南，帮助你处理插座、灯或空调的报修。

The question

“The socket, light, or air conditioner in my room is not working. How do I request a repair?”

🔧How to request a repair at our university

At Suzhou Polytechnic University you can report a broken socket, light or air conditioner through the channels below. The campus repair platform is the most recommended.

1 · Campus repair platform （校园报修平台）

Report online through the campus service hall (校园办事大厅) on the school's website or app, or the logistics service WeChat account. Choose your building, floor and room number, describe the fault and upload a photo if possible. The system creates a repair order, and you can track it from dispatch to completion.

2 · Dormitory duty room （宿舍值班室）

Every dormitory unit has a 24-hour duty station. For urgent faults, go in person or call the duty room — this is the fastest way outside working hours.

3 · Logistics service center hotline （总务处 / 后勤服务中心）

Call 0512-66503924 (extension 83924) on working days, 8:15–16:30. This is the general line for logistics and repairs — the staff can direct you to the right channel.

4 · International Exchange & Cooperation Office （国际交流合作处）

International students can ask this office for help: call 0512-66503820 or email international@jsspu.edu.cn.

⏱ Response standards: emergency repairs — on site within about 20 minutes; simple repairs — completed the same day; general repairs — completed within one day.

📋What happens next

  1. You submit the request (online, in person, or by phone).
  2. The maintenance department reviews the order (接单) and assigns a worker.
  3. A worker comes to your room and fixes the fault.
  4. You check the result and confirm (or rate) the repair when it is done.

⚡China's electricity — quick facts

  • Voltage: 220 V
  • Frequency: 50 Hz
  • Sockets / plugs: Type A, C and I — Type I (three flat pins in a “V”) is the most common.
⚠ Check your devices first. If you brought a charger or appliance from a 100–120 V country (e.g. the US or Japan), make sure it says “100–240 V” — most phone and laptop chargers do. If it only says “120 V”, you need a voltage converter; plugging it in directly can damage it.
💡 Before reporting a “broken” socket: try another device in the same socket, and check whether a circuit breaker in your room has tripped (push the switch back to ON). This often fixes the problem without waiting for a repair.

问题

“我房间里的插座、灯或者空调不工作了，我该如何申请报修？”

🔧在我们学校如何报修

在苏州职业技术大学，你可以通过以下渠道报修插座、灯或空调，推荐优先使用校园报修平台。

1 · 校园报修平台 （校园报修平台）

通过学校官网或 App 的校园办事大厅、或后勤服务公众号在线报修。选择楼栋、楼层、房间号，描述故障并尽可能上传照片。系统会生成维修单，你可以在线跟踪从派单到完成的整个过程。

2 · 宿舍值班室 （宿舍值班室）

每栋宿舍楼都设有 24 小时值班室。遇到紧急故障可当面报修或拨打值班室电话——这是非工作时间最快的报修方式。

3 · 总务处（后勤服务中心）热线 （总务处 / 后勤服务中心）

工作日 8:15–16:30 可拨打 0512-66503924（内线 83924）。这是后勤与维修的通用电话，工作人员会指引你到正确的报修渠道。

4 · 国际交流合作处 （国际交流合作处）

国际学生可向该部门求助：拨打 0512-66503820 或发邮件至 international@jsspu.edu.cn。

⏱ 维修响应标准：应急维修约 20 分钟到场；简单维修不过夜；一般维修不过天。

📋接下来的流程

  1. 你提交报修申请（线上、当面或电话）。
  2. 维修部门审核订单（接单）并派单给维修师傅。
  3. 维修师傅上门维修。
  4. 修好后你确认结果（有些平台还可以评价）。

⚡中国用电常识

  • 电压：220 V
  • 频率：50 Hz
  • 插座 / 插头类型：A、C、I 型——其中 I 型（三脚扁插，呈“V”形）最常见。
⚠ 先检查你的电器。如果你从 100–120 V 的国家（如美国、日本）带了充电器或电器，确认它标着 “100–240 V”（大多数手机、笔记本充电器都支持）。如果只标 “120 V”，就需要电压转换器，直接插会烧坏设备。
💡 报修“插座坏了”之前：先用另一个电器试试同一个插座，并检查房间里的空气开关是否跳闸了（把开关拨回 ON）。这样常常不用等维修就能解决问题。
